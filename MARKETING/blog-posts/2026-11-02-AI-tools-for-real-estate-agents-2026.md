## The Death of the "One-Size-Fits-All" Listing Description

Buyers in 2026 have seen it all. They are immune to the "Beautiful 3 bed, 2 bath" description. They expect a *story*, not a spec sheet. Generic copy makes you invisible. Specific, emotional copy gets you the phone call.
