# How to Teach AI Your Real Estate Brand Voice (In 10 Minutes)

Every agent wants to sound like themselves. Not like a template. Not like the agent down the street. Not like ChatGPT on autopilot.

But here's the truth: most agents don't *know* what their voice is. They've never written it down. They've never defined it.

If you can't describe your voice, neither can the AI.

Here's a 10-minute exercise that changes that — and makes your AI-generated listing copy sound like *you*.

---

## Step 1: Mine Your Best Listings (3 minutes)

Pull three listings that felt like *you*. The ones where you read them back and thought "yeah, that's exactly what I wanted to say."

For each one, copy three things:
- One sentence that felt perfect
- One word you use more than other agents
- One phrase you'd never say

**Example from a real agent:**
- Perfect sentence: "This is the kitchen where Sunday mornings happen."
- Signature word: "Light-filled" (appears in 80% of her listings)
- Never says: "Don't miss out" or "Must see"

---

## Step 2: Define Your Voice on Four Axes (4 minutes)

Rate yourself 1-10 on each:

| Axis | Score | What It Means |
|------|-------|---------------|
| Formal ↔ Casual | ___ | "The residence features" vs "This place has" |
| Minimal ↔ Descriptive | ___ | Short bullet points vs full narrative |
| Professional ↔ Warm | ___ | "Buyer to verify" vs "You're going to love this kitchen" |
| Traditional ↔ Bold | ___ | Standard descriptions vs playful/unexpected language |

**Real example from a top-producing Denver agent:**
- Formal/Casual: 7/10 (more casual)
- Minimal/Descriptive: 8/10 (very descriptive)
- Professional/Warm: 8/10 (very warm)
- Traditional/Bold: 6/10 (slightly bold)

Voice summary: "Warm, descriptive storyteller who talks to buyers like a friend showing them around."

---

## Step 3: Build Your Prompt Pack (3 minutes)

Take your voice profile and feed it as instructions:

> Write listing descriptions in a warm, descriptive voice. Use sensory details. Start each listing with a scene, not a feature list. Never say "don't miss out" or "must see." Preferred word: "light-filled." Write at an 8th-grade reading level. Keep paragraphs under 3 sentences. End with an invitation, not a command.

**That one prompt consistently produces copy that agents say sounds like them.**

---

## Step 4: Test and Iterate

Write one listing with your prompt. Read it aloud. Does it sound like you? If not, tweak:

- Too formal? Turn down the professional dial
- Too wordy? Lower the descriptive score
- Too casual? Pull back on warmth

Three rounds of iteration is usually enough.

---

## Why This Beats Generic AI

Generic ChatGPT copy sounds generic because it has no constraints. It's writing for everyone, which means it connects with no one.

A defined brand voice is a constraint system. It tells the AI:
- What to say
- What *not* to say
- How to say it
- Who you're talking to
